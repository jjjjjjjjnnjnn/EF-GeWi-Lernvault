Klausur-Satz: `Postcolonial identity emerges from the tension between inherited traditions and imposed colonial structures.`

## Schritt 2 — entdecken

PRETRAINING术语盒（核心5词，先读三遍英中，合书自测中文→英文，Evidenz：pretraining降认知负荷）：

中文在上，英文在下：

- 离散（族群） — diaspora: a community living outside its place of origin while keeping cultural ties to it.
- 他者化 — othering: portraying a group as fundamentally different and inferior in order to justify power over it.
- 混杂身份 — hybrid identity: an identity that blends elements of two or more cultures, often described as living "between worlds".
- 殖民遗产 — colonial legacy: lasting political, linguistic and cultural effects of colonial rule after independence.
- 第三文化 — third culture: a shared way of life that grows among people living between their parents' home culture and the host society.

Klausur-Satz: `Othering creates a hierarchy of "us" versus "them", while hybrid identity challenges this binary.`

## Schritt 3 — entdecken

ENTDECKEN（1概念 + 1文字图解）：

中文：后殖民视角只问一件事：谁有权力定义"正常"。殖民者通过 othering 把被殖民者写成"落后的他者"，于是英语、基督教学校、行政边界变成"文明标准"。独立后这些标准并未消失，而是和本土语言、宗教、家庭结构搅在一起，形成 hybrid identity：一个人可以同时用英语写论文、用约鲁巴语跟祖母开玩笑、周五去清真寺。Diaspora 把这种混杂带到伦敦、纽约，孩子在两种课本之间长大，"我是谁"变成每天的选择题。⏳ 待确认：若课堂第三文化案例不用 Nigeria，可将本段 Nigeria 例替换为 India 或 Kenya，概念链不变。

文字图解（ASCII 结构图，App支持解析渲染）：


Klausur-Satz: `Hybrid identity can be read as a creative answer to the colonial practice of othering.`

## Anekdote & Fun-Fact

**Anekdote / Fun-Fact (EN)**: In many Lagos markets, traders switch languages three times in one sentence — a greeting in Yoruba, a price in English, a joke in Pidgin. Linguists call this code-switching, and novelists imitate it to show identity in action.

**中文解读**: 一句话切三种语言不是"乱"，恰恰是混杂身份的现场直播：见什么人说什么话，身份跟着语码走。考试写作用上 code-switching 一词，立刻显得懂行。

**Bezug zum Konzept**: `Code-switching makes hybrid identity audible in everyday communication.`

## Schritt 4 — ausprobieren

BEISPIEL（正确例题示范，含教具操作与解答）：

[Werkzeug: highlighter]

AUFGABE (examine, AFB II): Examine how the invented vignette below presents hybrid identity. Highlight markers of diaspora, othering and hybridity (5 Nigeria facts are given; use at least three).

Vignette "Two Passports, One Kitchen" (original, 110 words): "Adaeze keeps two calendars in her London flat: the school term dates and her grandmother's market days in Enugu. At parents' evening she speaks polished English; on video calls she haggles in Igbo and laughs when her mother corrects her accent. A textbook once called her grandparents' village 'tribal and timeless' — she now writes in the margin: 'Who decides what is modern?' On Sundays jollof rice steams next to frozen pizza. 'I am not half of anything,' she says. 'I am double.'"

Nigeria in 5 facts (for Klausur use): 1. Most populous African country, over 200 million people. 2. More than 250 ethnic groups, largest: Hausa-Fulani, Yoruba, Igbo. 3. Former British colony, independent since 1960; English remains the official language. 4. Major religions: Islam in the north, Christianity in the south, plus indigenous traditions. 5. Large diaspora in Britain and the USA; Nollywood and Afrobeats spread its culture globally.

HILFE:
1. Schritt 1: Markiere Diaspora-Spuren (London flat, two calendars, video calls).
2. Schritt 2: Markiere Othering (textbook phrase "tribal and timeless" + margin question).
3. Schritt 3: Markiere Hybriditaet (polished English + Igbo, jollof + pizza, "I am double") und verknuepfe je einen Nigeria-Fakt.

MUSTERLÖSUNG: Diaspora appears in the split setting London/Enugu and the two calendars, matching fact 5 (large British diaspora). Othering appears in the textbook label "tribal and timeless", which freezes a dynamic Igbo community (fact 2) into a backward stereotype; Adaeze's margin question reverses the gaze and reclaims definition power. Hybrid identity appears in her code-switching (English/Igbo, fact 3: English as colonial legacy plus indigenous languages) and in the kitchen image (jollof + pizza): cultures are not halved but doubled. The closing line "I am double" turns hybridity from a deficit into a resource.

Klausur-Satz: `The vignette presents hybrid identity not as loss but as a conscious doubling of cultural resources.`

## Schritt 5 — ausprobieren

VERGLEICH辨别实验（双向辨析：描述身份 vs. 讨论价值）：

VERGLEICH: Wähle erst das Verfahren — 【选概念】先判断题目要的是 (i) Examine-Verfahren（examine：拆解文本如何呈现身份，答"怎么写的"）还是 (ii) Discuss-Verfahren（discuss：权衡"混杂是负担还是资源"，答"怎么看"）—— dann schreiben.

AUFGABE A：Examine how Adaeze's language use signals belonging.
AUFGABE B：Discuss whether hybrid identity is a burden or a resource.

HILFE: A nennt "how ... signals" -> Verfahren (i), Textbeleg + Konzept. B nennt "whether / burden or resource" -> Verfahren (ii), Pro + Contra + Urteil.【选概念：题干出现 how is X presented/portrayed 选 examine；出现 discuss / to what extent / burden vs resource 选正反合：先承认撕裂感，再论创造力，最后站队。】

ANTWORT: A erfordert Verfahren (i): Polished school English signals public belonging in Britain, Igbo haggling signals intimate belonging to the Enugu family; switching between them marks a flexible double membership. B erfordert Verfahren (ii): On the one hand, constant switching can feel exhausting and invite othering ("Where are you really from?"); on the other hand, double literacy — two languages, two calendars — widens career and cultural options. Overall, the text weighs the resource side more heavily: hybridity costs effort but pays in perspective.

Klausur-Satz: `Although hybridity demands daily negotiation, it ultimately offers a broader repertoire of belonging.`

## Fehlvorstellung

   中文纠偏：这是 deficit 视角。文本常写成 double 而非 half：多一套语言、多一套节日，是加法不是减法。考试要写 doubling 而非 lack。

   Korrektur-Satz: `Political independence does not end colonial legacy in language, education and borders.`
