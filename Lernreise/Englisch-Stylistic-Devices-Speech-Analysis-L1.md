## Schritt 1 — entdecken

ZIELE (3条，本节20分钟学完能做到——先读中文，再记英文)：

1. 中文：能认出并命名15个演讲常用修辞格，看到仿写演讲片段时能标出至少5处手法并说明其说服意图。
2. 中文：能用 P.E.E. 链（Point-Evidence-Explanation）写出完整的演讲分析段落，做到"论点—引证—解释效果"三步闭环。
3. 中文：能评价（evaluate）一篇100词仿写演讲的说服力，从 ethos/pathos/logos 任选一角写出 Klausur 级结论句（AFB II-III）。

Klausur-Satz: `The speaker employs stylistic devices to shape the audience's emotions and to strengthen the persuasiveness of the central argument.`

## Schritt 2 — entdecken

PRETRAINING术语盒（核心5词，先读三遍英中，合书自测中文→英文，Evidenz：pretraining降认知负荷）：

中文在上，英文在下：

- 修辞格 / 表现手法 — stylistic device: a deliberate language choice (e.g. repetition, metaphor) used to create a specific effect on the audience.
- 排比三段式 — tricolon: a series of three parallel phrases or clauses, often building up to a climax.
- 首语重复 — anaphora: repetition of the same word or phrase at the beginning of successive clauses.
- 说服三要素 — ethos, pathos, logos: credibility of the speaker, emotional appeal, and logical argument.
- P.E.E. 段落链 — P.E.E. chain (Point-Evidence-Explanation): a paragraph structure that states a point, quotes brief evidence, and explains its effect.

Klausur-Satz: `Each device can be analysed with the P.E.E. chain: a clear point, brief textual evidence, and an explanation of its effect on the audience.`

## Schritt 3 — entdecken

ENTDECKEN（1概念 + 1文字图解）：

中文：演讲分析的核心问题不是"用了什么"，而是"为什么有效"。记住公式：手法 + 位置 + 效果 = 分析。同样是重复，放在开头（anaphora）是敲鼓点、聚情绪；放在结尾（epiphora）是收网、留回音。三并列（tricolon）负责加速情绪，设问（rhetorical question）负责把听众拉进对话，反义词并置（antithesis）负责制造"我们 vs 他们"的张力。分析时永远三问：说话人想让听众感到什么（pathos）？相信什么（logos）？凭什么信他（ethos）？15个手法全是这三问的工具。

文字图解（ASCII 结构图，App支持解析渲染）：

```diagram
         SPEECH EFFECT = DEVICE + POSITION + PURPOSE
           +------------------+------------------+
           |  SOUND           |  MEANING         |
           |  alliteration    |  metaphor/simile |
           |  anaphora        |  hyperbole       |
           |  epiphora        |  personification |
           |  tricolon        |  emotive language|
           |  parallelism     |  antithesis      |
           +------------------+------------------+
           |  CONTACT         |  ARGUMENT        |
           |  rhetorical ?    |  inclusive "we"  |
           |  direct address  |  imperative      |
           |  repetition      |  (ethos/pathos/  |
           |                  |   logos)         |
           +------------------+------------------+
      P.E.E.: Point -> "quote" -> Effect on audience
```

Klausur-Satz: `The effect of a device depends on its position in the speech and on the speaker's persuasive purpose.`

## Anekdote & Fun-Fact

**Anekdote / Fun-Fact (EN)**: Speech coaches count beats, not just words. A tricolon works like three drum hits: the first states, the second strengthens, the third lands. That is why invented campaign lines so often come in threes — the brain remembers the third hit best.

**中文解读**: 三段式像三记鼓点：第一下摆观点，第二下加力，第三下落锤。人脑对"第三下"记忆最深，所以仿写演讲时凑满三并列，评卷人立刻能听到节奏感。

**Bezug zum Konzept**: `Rhythm supports rhetoric: patterns of three guide attention and make the climax memorable.`

## Schritt 4 — ausprobieren

BEISPIEL（正确例题示范，含教具操作与解答）：

[Werkzeug: highlighter]

AUFGABE (analyze, AFB II): Read the original 100-word model speech below (in the style of a civil-rights / wartime address, fully invented, no quotation from real speeches). Highlight 5 devices and analyse two of them in a P.E.E. paragraph.

Model speech "The Bridge We Build" (100 words, original): "We did not come here to wait. We came to walk, to work, to win. Will our children ask whether we stood aside? We will answer: we stood together. They tell us the river is too wide; I tell you we are the bridge. We, the patient, we, the brave, we, the many — can we fail if we move as one? Stand up, speak out, hold on. The night is dark, but our purpose is dawn. Let history record not our fear, but our first step."

HILFE:
1. Schritt 1: Markiere Klangmuster — suche tricolon ("to walk, to work, to win"), anaphora ("We ... We ..."), alliteration ("stood aside / stood together").
2. Schritt 2: Markiere Kontaktmittel — rhetorical question ("Will our children ask ...? / can we fail ...?"), imperative ("Stand up, speak out, hold on"), inclusive "we".
3. Schritt 3: Waehle zwei Fundstellen und baue je eine P.E.E.-Kette: Point (nennt Geraet + Zweck), Evidence (max. 8 Woerter zitieren), Explanation (Wirkung auf pathos/logos).

MUSTERLÖSUNG: Point: The tricolon "to walk, to work, to win" accelerates the rhythm. Evidence: three parallel infinitives in one line. Explanation: The rising beat builds collective energy (pathos) and presents action as inevitable, which strengthens the speaker's leadership ethos. Point 2: The rhetorical question "can we fail if we move as one?" involves the audience. Evidence: a question with no answer expected. Explanation: Listeners must silently answer "no", so they persuade themselves; this supports logos by turning unity into the logical condition of success. Inventory of all 15 devices in the speech: 1 tricolon, 2 anaphora, 3 epiphora-like echo (wait / win), 4 parallelism, 5 rhetorical question x2, 6 hyperbole ("we are the bridge"), 7 metaphor ("purpose is dawn"), 8 simile-free comparison via antithesis (night vs dawn), 9 personification ("history record"), 10 alliteration, 11 antithesis (too wide / bridge; fear / first step), 12 repetition ("we"), 13 emotive language (brave, dark, dawn), 14 inclusive pronoun "we", 15 imperative ("Stand up ...").

Klausur-Satz: `By combining tricolon and rhetorical questions, the speaker turns passive listeners into active supporters of the cause.`

## Schritt 5 — ausprobieren

VERGLEICH辨别实验（双向辨析：罗列手法 vs. 评价效果）：

VERGLEICH: Wähle erst das Verfahren — 【选概念】先判断题目动词要的是 (i) Analyse-Verfahren（analyze：指出手法+引用+解释效果，答"如何说服"）还是 (ii) Evaluation-Verfahren（evaluate：判断说服力+权衡正反+结论，答"有多有效、局限何在"）—— dann schreiben.

AUFGABE A：Analyze how the speaker uses inclusive language in "we, the patient, we, the brave, we, the many".
AUFGABE B：Evaluate whether the speech is convincing for a present-day school audience.

HILFE: A nennt "how" + ein Mittel -> Verfahren (i), P.E.E.-Kette. B nennt "whether / convincing" -> Verfahren (ii), Urteil + Abwaegung.【选概念：题干出现 how + 手法名 选分析链；出现 how convincing / evaluate / to what extent 选评价式：先承认效果，再指出局限（如 overgeneralisation, emotional pressure），最后下结论。】

ANTWORT: A erfordert Verfahren (i): Point — inclusive anaphora builds unity; Evidence — "we, the patient, we, the brave"; Explanation — repetition of "we" erases distance between speaker and audience and creates collective pathos. B erfordert Verfahren (ii): The speech is largely convincing because rhythm and direct address create urgency; its limit is that metaphors ("we are the bridge") inspire but offer no concrete policy (logos gap). Overall, it moves the audience emotionally more than it convinces logically.

Klausur-Satz: `While the inclusive language effectively creates solidarity, its persuasive power remains mainly emotional rather than logical.`

## Schritt 6 — check

CHECK检索默写（自测 3 题，与答案配对）：

FRAGE: What are the three steps of a P.E.E. paragraph in speech analysis? | ANTWORT: Point (name device + purpose), Evidence (short quote, max. 8 words), Explanation (effect on audience in terms of ethos/pathos/logos).
FRAGE: What is the difference between anaphora and tricolon? | ANTWORT: Anaphora repeats the same opening across clauses ("We came ... We will ..."); tricolon is a group of three parallel elements ("walk, work, win") — both create rhythm, but anaphora binds, tricolon climaxes.
FRAGE: Name one device for each persuasive appeal (ethos, pathos, logos). | ANTWORT: Ethos — inclusive "we" and confident imperatives signal leadership; pathos — emotive language and metaphor stir feelings; logos — antithesis and rhetorical questions frame unity as the logical choice.

Klausur-Satz: `A complete analysis always connects the quoted device to its concrete effect on the audience.`

## Fehlvorstellung

(非Schritt小节，Parser 自动识别，不计入步骤步数)

1. 误解"把手法名字列出来就算分析完了"。
   中文纠偏：只贴标签是 AFB I，上不了分。必须跟一句效果：让听众感到什么、相信什么。没有 Explanation 的 P.E.E. 只有 P.E.，等于没写完。
   Korrektur-Satz: `Naming a device without explaining its effect on the audience does not constitute an analysis.`

2. 误解"修辞问句需要回答"。
   中文纠偏：恰恰相反，rhetorical question 的力量在于"不用回答"。听众在心里自动回答，就等于自己说服了自己。写分析时点明"expected answer"即可。
   Korrektur-Satz: `A rhetorical question is effective precisely because the audience supplies the intended answer silently.`

## Schritt 8 — entdecken

TAKEAWAY 1盒（核心总结）：

中文：手法是工具，效果是得分点。看到演讲先标三类：声音类（头韵/首重复/三并列）、意义类（比喻/夸张/对比）、接触类（设问/呼语/祈使）。每处分析只写 P.E.E. 三句：点名手法+引半句原文+说清 pathos/logos/ethos 效果。遇到 evaluate，先夸后批再结论：情感强、逻辑弱是万能起点。
Takeaway-Satz: `Devices create rhythm, images and contact; analysis proves their effect, evaluation weighs their limits.`
