## Schritt 1 — entdecken

ZIELE (3条，本节20分钟学完能做到——先读中文，再记英文)：

1. 中文：能用对比矩阵说清两部反乌托邦的控制逻辑：1984 靠监视与恐惧，Brave New World 靠娱乐与欲望。
2. 中文：能比较（compare）两部作品在语言、权力、个人自由三维度上的异同，各写一句 Klausur-Satz（AFB II）。
3. 中文：能评估（assess）"哪种未来更危险"，写出带权衡的结论段（AFB III）。

Klausur-Satz: `Both novels warn against total control, but they imagine opposite instruments: fear versus pleasure.`

## Schritt 2 — entdecken

PRETRAINING术语盒（核心5词，先读三遍英中，合书自测中文→英文，Evidenz：pretraining降认知负荷）：

中文在上，英文在下：

- 反乌托邦 — dystopia: an imagined society that appears ordered but systematically destroys freedom.
- 监视 — surveillance: constant observation that forces people to censor themselves.
- 娱乐至死式控制 — control through entertainment: power that works by distracting people with pleasure so they stop asking questions.
- 语言即权力 — language as power: the idea that limiting words limits what people can think.
- 对比矩阵 — comparison matrix: a table that compares two texts across fixed categories (power, language, freedom).

Klausur-Satz: `Surveillance controls from the outside, entertainment controls from the inside.`

## Schritt 3 — entdecken

ENTDECKEN（1概念 + 1文字图解）：

中文：两本书是同一道题的两个答案：人是怎么失去自由的？1984 答"被看着"：到处是眼睛，人先自我审查，再自我告密，语言被切碎（仿写标语式短句即可，不抄原文），思想无词可用。Brave New World 答"被哄着"：人人有乐子、有新药、有消费，舒服到不想反抗，自由不是被抢走的，是被"爽"换走的。一句话记忆：一个用恐惧让你不敢想，一个用快乐让你不想想。全原创转述，不引原文长句。

文字图解（ASCII 结构图，App支持解析渲染）：

```diagram
              HOW FREEDOM IS LOST
              /                 \
   1984: SURVEILLANCE      BRAVE NEW WORLD: ENTERTAINMENT
   watch -> fear ->        distract -> desire ->
   self-censorship         indifference
        |                        |
   language narrowed        language flooded
   (few words left)         (many empty words)
        \                        /
         v                      v
     same result: citizens stop questioning power
```

Klausur-Satz: `Fear narrows thought, pleasure empties it; both end obedience.`

## Anekdote & Fun-Fact

**Anekdote / Fun-Fact (EN)**: Teachers joke that one novel scares you into airplane mode, the other into doomscrolling. After 1984 readers cover their webcams; after Brave New World they check their screen time — and feel caught by both.

**中文解读**: 玩笑话点出真相：读完1984你想遮摄像头，读完美丽新世界你想查屏幕时长——一个防"被看"，一个防"被哄"。考试举这个 contemporary 例子，assess 段立刻有现实感。

**Bezug zum Konzept**: `Surveillance fears the eye above; entertainment fears no eye because viewers volunteer their attention.`

## Schritt 4 — ausprobieren

BEISPIEL（正确例题示范，含教具操作与解答）：

[Werkzeug: balance]

AUFGABE (compare, AFB II): Complete the comparison matrix for the two novels across power, language and freedom. Use only paraphrase (no long quotations); invented short slogans in the style of each regime are allowed.

HILFE:
1. Schritt 1: Power — 1984: open watcher + punishment; Brave New World: hidden managers + rewards. Frage: Wer bestraft, wer belohnt?
2. Schritt 2: Language — 1984: shrink vocabulary so dissent lacks words; Brave New World: flood with slogans and feelies so reflection lacks time. Frage: Weniger Woerter oder mehr Ablenkung?
3. Schritt 3: Freedom — beide nehmen Freiheit, aber 1984 erzeugt Gehorsam durch Angst, Brave New World durch Zufriedenheit. Schreibe je Kategorie einen Satz.

MUSTERLÖSUNG: Power: In 1984 control is visible and punitive — citizens obey because they feel watched and fear punishment. In Brave New World control is invisible and rewarding — citizens obey because comfort, status pills and leisure make obedience feel like choice. Language: The 1984 regime narrows language so that critical thought finds no words (paraphrase: a slogan-style claim that loyalty equals truthfulness). The Brave New World regime floods language with catchy slogans and constant amusement so that critical thought finds no silence. Freedom: Both destroy political freedom, but by opposite paths — one through terror that forbids thinking differently, one through pleasure that removes the wish to think differently. Matrix in one line per cell is enough for full marks if each cell names instrument + effect.

Klausur-Satz: `Visible surveillance punishes dissent, while engineered pleasure prevents dissent from arising.`

## Schritt 5 — ausprobieren

VERGLEICH辨别实验（双向辨析：比较异同 vs. 评估危险）：

VERGLEICH: Wähle erst das Verfahren — 【选概念】先判断题目要的是 (i) Compare-Verfahren（compare：列异同+各配一例，答"哪里一样哪里不一样"）还是 (ii) Assess-Verfahren（assess：权衡哪种更危险+当代联系+结论，答"哪个更糟、为什么"）—— dann schreiben.

AUFGABE A：Compare the role of technology in both novels.
AUFGABE B：Assess which future is more dangerous for today's readers.

HILFE: A nennt "compare the role" -> Verfahren (i), Matrix-Satz + Beleg. B nennt "assess which ... more dangerous" -> Verfahren (ii), Pro + Contra + Urteil + Gegenwart.【选概念：题干出现 compare / similarities and differences 选矩阵三行；出现 assess / which is more dangerous / to what extent 选评价式：先承认一方的可怕，再论另一方更隐蔽，最后落到当代（camera vs feed）。】

ANTWORT: A erfordert Verfahren (i): In both novels technology serves power, but differently — telescreen-style watchers enforce conformity through fear, while pleasure technologies (engineered leisure, mood products) enforce conformity through satisfaction. B erfordert Verfahren (ii): The surveillance future is more openly brutal and therefore easier to recognise and resist; the entertainment future is more dangerous for today's readers because it feels voluntary — endless feeds also reward self-distraction. Overall, pleasure-control is the nearer danger, since readers already trade attention for comfort daily.

Klausur-Satz: `Terror warns its victims, pleasure recruits them; the unnoticed danger is harder to resist.`

## Schritt 6 — check

CHECK检索默写（自测 3 题，与答案配对）：

FRAGE: State the core contrast between 1984 and Brave New World in one sentence. | ANTWORT: 1984 controls through surveillance and fear from the outside; Brave New World controls through entertainment and desire from the inside.
FRAGE: Give one matrix line for language in each novel. | ANTWORT: 1984 — vocabulary is narrowed so dissent lacks words; Brave New World — attention is flooded so reflection lacks time.
FRAGE: What structure fits an assess question on "which future is more dangerous"? | ANTWORT: Admit the horror of one side, argue the hidden danger of the other, link to the present (cameras vs feeds), then judge with reasons.

Klausur-Satz: `Both regimes abolish freedom, but only one lets citizens notice the loss.`

## Fehlvorstellung

(非Schritt小节，Parser 自动识别，不计入步骤步数)

1. 误解"两本书都在讲科技坏，所以可以混着写"。
   中文纠偏：科技只是工具，控制逻辑相反：一个靠"怕"（watch-punish），一个靠"爽"（reward-distract）。混写等于把矩阵两列合并，得分减半。
   Korrektur-Satz: `Technology must not be compared as such; the opposing logics of fear and pleasure must be kept apart.`

2. 误解"评估题只要站队说一个更可怕就行"。
   中文纠偏：assess 要权衡。只骂一方是 AFB II，承认"另一方也可怕、但这一方更贴近当代"才是 AFB III。必须有让步句。
   Korrektur-Satz: `An assessment requires weighing both dangers before judging which is more relevant today.`

## Schritt 8 — reflexion: Takeaway & Reflexion

TAKEAWAY 1盒（核心总结）：

中文：矩阵三行背下来：权力（明罚 vs 暗赏）、语言（词少 vs 声多）、自由（不敢想 vs 不想想）。compare 答异同各一句，assess 答"都可怕、但哄比吓更近"：监视易察觉，娱乐难察觉，当代例子用摄像头 vs 信息流。结尾金句：Terror warns, pleasure recruits。
Takeaway-Satz: `Fear forbids thought, pleasure removes its occasion — compare the instruments, assess the nearer danger.`
