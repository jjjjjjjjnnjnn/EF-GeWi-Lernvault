Klausur-Satz: `Shakespeare reveals inner guilt through language: what characters hide from others, they confess to the audience.`

## Schritt 2 — entdecken

PRETRAINING术语盒（核心5词，先读三遍英中，合书自测中文→英文，Evidenz：pretraining降认知负荷）：

中文在上，英文在下：

- 独白 — soliloquy: a speech delivered alone on stage that reveals a character's inner thoughts to the audience.
- 旁白 — aside: a brief remark to the audience that other characters on stage supposedly do not hear.
- 无韵五步诗 — blank verse: unrhymed lines of roughly ten syllables that sound close to natural speech but keep a steady rhythm.
- 戏剧反讽 — dramatic irony: the audience knows something the characters do not, which creates suspense or pity.
- 早期现代词汇桥 — Early Modern bridge (thou/hath/doth): thou = you (subject), thee = you (object), thy/thine = your, hath = has, doth = does.

Klausur-Satz: `Soliloquy and aside give the audience privileged knowledge, which is the basis of dramatic irony.`

## Schritt 3 — entdecken

ENTDECKEN（1概念 + 1文字图解）：

中文：莎剧的"偷听结构"就一句话：观众永远比台上人知道得多。独白是角色关起门跟观众说真话，旁白是开着门偷偷跟观众递纸条，无韵诗体是让真话听起来像心跳（十音节 steady beat），戏剧反讽是观众拿着答案看角色交白卷——比如我们知道手上的"血点"洗不掉，梦游的人却还在拼命擦。全原创仿写即可，不背全剧：抓住"谁知道、谁不知道"就能得分。以下只引公有领域短句（首演/首印四百年前，版权早已过期），每句配现代转述。

文字图解（ASCII 结构图，App支持解析渲染）：

```diagram
          AUDIENCE (knows most)
          ^                  ^
          | soliloquy        | aside ("you hear, they don't")
          |                  |
     [alone on stage]   [whisper to audience]
          |                  |
          +--------+---------+
                   |
            blank verse (heartbeat rhythm)
                   |
           dramatic irony = audience knows MORE
                    than the character
```

Klausur-Satz: `Privileged speech forms create an information gap between audience and character.`

## Anekdote & Fun-Fact

**Anekdote / Fun-Fact (EN)**: Actors call the sleepwalking scene a "nightmare solo": no dialogue partner, only a candle, a spot, and short broken lines. The shorter the line, the heavier the guilt — Shakespeare writes panic by breaking the verse.

**中文解读**: 梦游戏是"噩梦独角戏"：没有对手，只有蜡烛、斑点和断掉的诗行。诗行越短，罪疚越重——莎士比亚用"破句"写恐慌，看到短句碎句就往 guilt 上靠。

**Bezug zum Konzept**: `Broken rhythm signals a broken mind: fragmented lines mirror fragmented conscience.`

## Schritt 4 — ausprobieren

BEISPIEL（正确例题示范，含教具操作与解答）：

[Werkzeug: highlighter]

AUFGABE (analyze, AFB II): Analyse the public-domain mini-passage below (Macbeth, sleepwalking scene, heavily shortened, original spelling modernised). Mark soliloquy traits and one image, then write a P.E.E. chain.

Passage (public domain, short quotes + modern paraphrase): Lady Macbeth, alone with a candle, rubs her hands: "Out, damned spot! out, I say!" [Modern: Get off, cursed stain!] "What is done cannot be undone." [Modern: What happened cannot be reversed.] "Will these hands ever be clean?" [Modern: Will my guilt ever wash away?]

HILFE:
1. Schritt 1: Form bestimmen — allein auf der Buehne + Kerze + Selbstgespraech = soliloquy (kein aside, kein Partner).
2. Schritt 2: Sprache markieren — Imperativ "Out ... out", Wiederholung, Frage "Will ...?", Bild "spot / hands".
3. Schritt 3: Bruecke bauen — kein thou-Test noetig, aber Wortcheck: "What is done" = modern passive; "damned" = cursed. Schreibe P.E.E.: Point (guilt), Evidence (max. 8 Woerter), Explanation (Wirkung).

MUSTERLÖSUNG: Point: The imperatives "Out, damned spot! out, I say!" expose lasting guilt. Evidence: doubled "out" plus exclamation. Explanation: She scrubs an invisible stain before the audience, so private remorse becomes visible; the spot image turns moral guilt into a physical mark that no washing removes. Point 2: The question "Will these hands ever be clean?" deepens the reading: its expected answer "never" admits that rank and power cannot undo murder. Vocabulary bridge: "damned" = cursed (modern), "spot" = stain/guilt-mark, "What is done cannot be undone" = the past is irreversible. Blank-verse note: the lines are short and broken, which mirrors a disturbed mind rather than flowing reason.

Klausur-Satz: `The repeated imperatives turn invisible guilt into a visible stage image.`

## Schritt 5 — ausprobieren

VERGLEICH辨别实验（双向辨析：说什么 vs. 意味着什么）：

VERGLEICH: Wähle erst das Verfahren — 【选概念】先判断题目要的是 (i) Analyse-Verfahren（analyze：拆形式+引证+说效果，答"怎么写的"）还是 (ii) Interpretations-Verfahren（interpret：由词句推出人物/主题判断，答"意味着什么"）—— dann schreiben.

AUFGABE A：Analyze the function of the aside in an invented Romeo-style line: "[Aside] I smile at the feast, yet my heart kneels elsewhere."
AUFGABE B：Interpret what the line reveals about love and loyalty.

HILFE: A nennt "function of the aside" -> Verfahren (i), Form + Wissensvorsprung. B nennt "what ... reveals" -> Verfahren (ii), Deutung + Thema.【选概念：题干出现 function / how is X used 选分析；出现 interpret / what does X reveal / suggest about 选阐释：先转述大意，再连主题（love vs duty, appearance vs truth）。】

ANTWORT: A erfordert Verfahren (i): The aside gives the audience secret knowledge — the smile is public mask, the kneeling heart is private truth — which only we hear; this split creates dramatic irony for the following feast dialogue. B erfordert Verfahren (ii): The line suggests love as divided loyalty: social duty demands a smile, inner feeling demands devotion. It supports the theme that in both plays passion collides with an order (family honour, royal power) that cannot contain it.

Klausur-Satz: `While analysis describes the aside as a channel of secret knowledge, interpretation reads it as proof of divided loyalty.`

## Schritt 8 — reflexion: Takeaway & Reflexion

TAKEAWAY 1盒（核心总结）：

中文：先数人再定名：一人在场长剖白是独白，多人在场偷递一句是旁白；观众比角色多知道一点就是反讽。梦游句只记三件套：Out（命令+重复）+ spot（罪= stain）+ 问句（Will...? 答案是否定的）。thou=你，hath=has，doth=does，转述完必须跟一句"这说明……"才算 interpret。
Takeaway-Satz: `Count who is on stage, bridge the old words, then turn language into meaning: guilt made visible.`
