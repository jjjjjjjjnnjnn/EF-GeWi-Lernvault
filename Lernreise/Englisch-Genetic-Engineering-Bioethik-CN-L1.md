## Schritt 5 — ausprobieren: Verfahrensvergleich & Abgrenzung

VERGLEICH辨别实验（双向辨析：利弊权衡 vs 立场论证）：

VERGLEICH: Waehle erst das Verfahren — 【选程序】先看动词：(i) 利弊权衡（discuss/weigh：两边对等、末段裁决）oder (ii) 立场论证（argue/support：一段式辩护、反驳前置）—— dann loesen.

AUFGABE A：Discuss the pros and cons of GM crops.
AUFGABE B：Argue in favour of banning designer babies.

HILFE: A 含 pros and cons → Verfahren (i)。B 含 in favour of → Verfahren (ii)。【选程序：见权衡摆两边；见立场先让步。】

ANTWORT: A erfordert Verfahren (i): Balanced paragraphs (yield/pesticide vs ecology/patent), verdict only at the end by criterion food security vs precaution. B erfordert Verfahren (ii): Clear thesis first, strongest counter (parental freedom) conceded early, then rebutted by dignity and equality, thesis restated — one-sided by design. Mixing them loses task achievement: neutrality in B looks evasive, partisanship in A looks biased.

Klausur-Satz: `Discuss balances then judges, argue judges then defends.`
