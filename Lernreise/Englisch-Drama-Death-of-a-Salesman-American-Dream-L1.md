# Lernreise: Arthur Miller: Death of a Salesman – The Fallacy of the American Dream (L1, Ziel Klausur)

## Schritt 1 — entdecken: The Tired Sample Cases in Brooklyn
ZIELE (3条，本节15分钟学完能做到——先读中文，再记德语/英语)：

1. 中文：能说清阿瑟·米勒（Arthur Miller）名剧《推销员之死》（Death of a Salesman, 1949）对资本主义“美国梦”（American Dream）异化为纯粹消费主义与身份地位虚荣的深刻解构。
2. 中文：能深度分析主角威利·洛曼（Willy Loman: "Low-man"）的双重悲剧心理机制：拒绝承认平庸现实的妄想症（Delusion of Grandeur）、将“受人喜爱”（"being well-liked"）误认为成功圣经，以及笛声（Flute motif）与闪回倒叙（Expressionistic Flashbacks）的舞台表意。
3. 中文：能在高中英语统考戏剧分析大题（AFB I/II/III）中对比长子比夫（Biff Loman）的觉醒与次子哈皮（Happy Loman）的执迷，评注落幕挽歌（Requiem: "A man is not a piece of fruit!"）对现代劳动者尊严的呼号。

### Hook / Phaenomen

Late at night in a cramped, fragile frame house in Brooklyn, New York. Outside, towering concrete apartment buildings and cold neon billboards choke off the sky and suffocate the tiny backyard where not even a seed of grass can grow anymore. An exhausted sixty-three-year-old travelling salesman named Willy Loman drags two heavy sample cases through the front door. His feet ache, his hands tremble, and his mind wanders between painful hallucinations of the past and the humiliating reality of the present. For more than thirty years, Willy has driven thousands of miles across New England, believing with religious fanaticism in the gospel of the American Dream: that anyone who is handsome, athletic, and "well-liked" will effortlessly climb the ladder from rags to riches. But reality is brutal: his commissions have dwindled to zero, his refrigerator breaks down before it is paid off, his boss – a arrogant young man half his age – fires him without a dime of severance pay, and Willy is forced to beg his neighbour for charity just to pay his insurance premium. Why does a man who gave his entire life to the capitalist machine end up completely destroyed by the very myth he worshipped?

`Klausur-Satz: Arthur Miller deconstructs the capitalist perversion of the American Dream by demonstrating how Willy Loman internalizes an illusion of personality and material success that inevitably culminates in total self-alienation and suicidal sacrifice.`

## Schritt 2 — entdecken: The Critical Dramaturgical Glossary
PRETRAINING术语盒（核心4词，先读三遍中英，合书自测中文→英语）：

- 蜕变的美国梦 — The Corrupted American Dream: 原本追求自由、平等与个人潜能自我实现的清教徒崇高理想，退化为追求金钱、浮华外表与被他人盲目崇拜的消费主义神话。 The degradation of the original Jeffersonian ideal of freedom and spiritual fulfilment into a cut-throat pursuit of wealth, consumer goods, and social status.
- 威利·洛曼的姓氏象征 — The Name "Loman" (Low-man): 剧作家阿瑟·米勒精妙的文字游戏，暗示主角既是一个挣扎在社会最底层的小人物，又代表了每一个被体制碾碎的普通人。 The symbolic wordplay designating Willy as an ordinary "low man" crushed by an indifferent economic hierarchy.
- 表现主义舞台流动法 — Mobile Concurrency: 摒弃传统场景切换，让现实空间、回忆与幻觉在舞台上同一时刻无缝交织的创新舞台技法。 Miller's staging technique where past memories, hallucinations, and present realities fluidly merge on the same physical set.
- 挽歌与安葬幕 — The Requiem: 戏剧尾声在威利墓地前的挽歌场景，揭示了不同家庭成员面对悲剧所做出的根本性人生分歧。 The final epilogue scene at Willy's grave where the family members articulate their divergent conclusions about Willy's tragic existence.

## Schritt 4 — ausprobieren: The Close Reading Workshop on the Firing Scene

[Werkzeug: highlighter]

AUFGABE (analyze & characterize, AFB I/II):
Examine the dramatic confrontation between Willy Loman and his young employer Howard Wagner (Act 2):
Willy cries out desperately:
"You can't eat the orange and throw the peel away — a man is not a piece of fruit! Now pay attention to me, Howard! I've been with this firm thirty-four years, and now I can't pay my insurance! [...] You can't just drop me!"
1. Analyze the metaphor of the "orange and peel" and explain what it reveals about the relationship between employer and employee in industrial capitalism.
2. Characterize Howard Wagner's behaviour (listening to his new wire recorder, ignoring Willy, calling him "kid") and evaluate the cynicism of the modern corporate world.

MUSTERLOESUNG:
1. Analysis of the Orange Metaphor:
   - The central metaphor: An orange is bought for its sweet juice (the productive prime years of a worker's youth). Once the juice is squeezed dry, the tough, bitter peel (the aged, exhausted worker) is casually discarded into the trash.
   - Willy passionately rebels against this instrumentalization: "A man is not a piece of fruit!" He demands that thirty-four years of loyalty, human labour, and personal dedication must constitute an inalienable ethical bond that cannot simply be terminated because his short-term profitability has declined.
2. Characterization of Howard Wagner:
   - Howard represents the new generation of ruthless, technocratic corporate managers. He is utterly obsessed with his new technological toy (the wire recorder), enthusiastically listening to recordings of his children and wife while completely tuning out Willy's desperate pleas for bread.
   - He patronizingly calls the sixty-three-year-old Willy "kid", reversing the natural hierarchy of age, and fires him without a shred of empathy: "I don't want you representing us. I've been meaning to tell you for a long time." His politeness is merely a superficial veneer masking an ice-cold capitalist calculation.

## Schritt 5 — ausprobieren: Duel of the Sons: Awakening vs. Blind Continuation

VERGLEICH: Biff's Epiphany vs. Happy's Stubbornness (选概念)

- Position A (Biff Loman / The Awakened Son):
  - Epiphany: "I'm a dime a dozen, Pop, and so are you! I am not a leader of men, Willy, and neither are you. We never told the truth for ten minutes in this house!"
  - Redemption: He breaks free from the delusion, rejects urban consumerism, and chooses honest, manual outdoor work in the American West.
- Position B (Happy Loman / The Lost Follower):
  - Stubbornness at the grave: "Willy Loman did not die in vain. He had a good dream. It's the only dream you can have — to come out number-one man. He fought it out here, and this is where I'm gonna win it for him."
  - Tragedy: Happy learns nothing; he internalizes his father's toxic competitive myth and is doomed to repeat Willy's hollow cycle of adultery, loneliness, and failure.

Entscheidungsregel fuer die Klausur:
When discussing the ending in the "Requiem", contrast Linda's final words ("We're free... We're free and clear") with Willy's death: The house is paid off on the very day there is nobody left to live in it – the supreme irony of the bourgeois dream!

## Schritt 7 — check: Format-Check & Scoring

30 XP Schnelltest zur Verstaendigungssicherung:

FRAGE: Why did Biff abandon his high school graduation and give up on his promising future?
ANTWORT: He travelled to Boston to ask his father for help after failing mathematics, where he caught Willy in a hotel room in an adulterous affair with an anonymous woman ("The Woman"). The discovery that his heroic father was a liar and a cheat shattered Biffs trust and self-worth.

FRAGE: What is the tragic significance of Linda Lomans line "We're free and clear" at the end of the play?
ANTWORT: It refers to paying the final instalment of the thirty-year mortgage on their house; the tragedy is that they now own the empty house, but Willy is dead and the family has completely fragmented.
