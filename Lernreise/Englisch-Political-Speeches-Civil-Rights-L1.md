### Hook / Phaenomen

On August 28, 1963, more than 250,000 people gathered peacefully under the scorching sun in front of the Lincoln Memorial in Washington, D.C. Tension was palpable; military units were stationed nearby on high alert, and critics anticipated violent clashes. When Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. stepped up to the microphone, he did not deliver an angry manifesto of retribution or military confrontation. Instead, he spoke like a poet and a biblical prophet, using rhythm, cadenced repetition, and deeply rooted American ideals to transform the Lincoln Memorial into a national sanctuary of conscience. When he reached the climactic vision of his speech, he set aside his prepared notes and spoke straight from the heart, painting a radiant future where children would be judged not by the color of their skin but by the content of their character. This single speech did not merely address a gathering; it permanently altered the linguistic, moral, and legislative trajectory of modern democracy.

`Klausur-Satz: Martin Luther King Jr. masterfully employs a triadic synthesis of ethos, pathos, and biblical imagery to elevate the African American civil rights struggle into a universal imperative of democratic renewal.`

## Schritt 2 — entdecken: The Rhetorical Arsenal of Great Orators
PRETRAINING术语盒（核心4词，先读三遍中英，合书自测中文→英语）：

- 首语重复 / 首字排比 — Anaphora: 连续句子或分句开头重复相同词语以强化节奏与情感感染力的修辞手法。 The deliberate repetition of a word or phrase at the beginning of successive clauses to build emotional intensity and rhythmic resonance.
- 拓展隐喻 — Extended Metaphor: 在整段文本或整篇演说中持续展开的系统性隐喻。 A metaphor that unfolds across multiple sentences or paragraphs to construct a coherent symbolic framework (e.g. comparing racial injustice to a "promissory note" that has defaulted).
- 情感诉求 — Pathos: 诉诸听众同理心、义愤、希望等深层情绪以达成说服目的的修辞机制。 The mode of persuasion that appeals directly to the audience's emotions, values, and shared collective memory.
- 互文指涉 / 典故 — Allusion: 对圣经、独立宣言、宪法或文学经典的隐蔽或明晰引用。 A concise reference to a historically, culturally, or religiously significant event or document to lend authoritative weight (Ethos) to the argument.

## Schritt 4 — ausprobieren: The Speech Analysis Workshop

[Werkzeug: text-analyse]

AUFGABE (analyze & explain, AFB I/II):
Examine the following excerpt from Dr. King's address:
"Five score years ago, a great American, in whose symbolic shadow we stand today, signed the Emancipation Proclamation. This momentous decree came as a great beacon light of hope to millions of Negro slaves who had been seared in the flames of withering injustice. [...] But 100 years later, the Negro still is not free."
1. Identify two distinct stylistic devices in this passage and explain their immediate functional effect on the listeners.
2. Analyze how Dr. King establishes his Ethos in the opening lines of the speech.

MUSTERLOESUNG:
1. Stylistic devices and function:
   - Archaic phrasing / Allusion ("Five score years ago"): By echoing Abraham Lincoln's Gettysburg Address ("Four score and seven years ago"), King immediately summons Lincoln's authority and places his own movement in direct continuity with the abolition of slavery.
   - Contrastive imagery / Metaphor ("beacon light of hope" vs. "flames of withering injustice"): The juxtaposition of benevolent guiding light against agonizing, destructive heat emotionally amplifies the moral depravity of segregation and the desperate longing for freedom.
2. Establishment of Ethos:
   - King grounds his credibility not in personal ambition, but in revered American history. By standing in Lincoln's "symbolic shadow," he acts not as an outside rebel, but as a custodian of founding constitutional promises, disarming accusations of un-American radicalism.

`Klausur-Satz: Bei Aufgaben zu Political Speeches and Rhetoric in the Civil Rights Movement muss die theoriegeleitete Begruendung stets durch exakte Fachtermini und empirische Belege abgesichert werden.`

## Schritt 5 — ausprobieren: Duel of Persuasion: Rational Argument vs. Emotional Appeal

VERGLEICH: Logos (Rational Argumentation) vs. Pathos (Emotional Appeal) (选概念)

- Position A (Logos / Rational Reasoning):
  - Focus: Facts, statistical evidence, legal obligations, cause-and-effect coherence.
  - Example in speech: The metaphor of the promissory note and constitutional guarantee.
  - Effect: Convinces the analytical mind that discrimination is an objective breach of contract.
- Position B (Pathos / Affective Resonance):
  - Focus: Shared values, sensory imagery, evocative tone, rhythm and climax.
  - Example in speech: The repeated refrain "I have a dream that one day..."
  - Effect: Inspires collective empathy, unites diverse listeners, and motivates active civic courage.

Entscheidungsregel fuer die Klausur:
When analyzing a political speech in an Abitur exam, never simply list stylistic devices; always explain how the orator strategically balances Logos (justifying the demand) with Pathos (mobilizing the audience) to achieve the communicative goal!
