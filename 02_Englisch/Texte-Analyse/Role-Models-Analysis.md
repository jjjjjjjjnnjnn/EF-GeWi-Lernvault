# Role Models: Summary, Analysis, Comment (Topic: Role Models, LIN, Sept 2026)

> 中文一句话：三任务链=先缩写（只转述不评论）→再分析（作者用了什么手段、达到什么效果）→最后评论（亮观点+给理由+举例子）；全程P.E.E.（观点—证据—解释）+自查表收尾。

## 1. 中文讲一遍 (Feynman)

- **Teil A三任务链与AFB对应**：Summary是AFB I（wiedergeben：把作者说了什么压缩转述）；Analysis是AFB II（anwenden/analysieren：作者是怎么做、手段有何效果）；Comment/Evaluation是AFB III（beurteilen：我怎么看、理由是什么）。三段顺序固定，不可提前评论。
- **Summary规则（自查表）**：首句四要素（text type + title + author + year/topic）；只收主要信息，例子/重复/细节全删，长度约原文1/4–1/3；全程own words，禁direct quotes/direct speech（转indirect speech）；时态simple present（可配present perfect）；linking words串逻辑；neutral + formal register（无缩写、无比喻、无个人观点）。交卷前逐项打勾：task focus是否扣住题目限定（如criticism/effects类限定词）、structure是否首句+逻辑主体、conciseness是否明显短于原文。
- **Analysis框架**：Introduction=一句话thesis（作者用什么手段达到什么读者效果）+判定文本类型（分享个人经历与观点→opinion piece）；Main body只分析题目点名的焦点，不面面俱到——(1) language/choice of words（keywords、word fields、connotations正负、register），(2) communicative strategies（quoting experts、examples、inclusive/personal pronouns、rhetorical questions、statistics、addressing "you"、imperatives），(3) line of argument/structure（introduction钩子→main body组织方式topical/antithetical/chronological/climactic→conclusion收束方式appeal/plea/quotation）；每点走P.E.E.三步：Point（点名手段）→Evidence（精确行号，直接引l.或改述cf. l.）→Explain（手段对读者的效果）；Conclusion只收束最重要的发现，不加新信息（Overall句型收尾）。
- **Comment/Evaluation写法**：150–200词，四段式 Introduction（引入topic+复述statement）→Position（明确表态）→Arguments（2–3个论点，每个先解释再举例，至少考虑一个counterargument/另一视角）→Conclusion（总结+final judgement）；时态simple present讨论一般性话题；linking words（firstly, moreover, however, on the other hand, although, therefore, in conclusion）；peer-feedback自查：观点清不清、论点是罗列还是解释了、有没有具体例子、两面都看到没有、结尾有无明确判断、语言（varied vocabulary、spelling/grammar/punctuation）。
- **课堂法一句话**：Mind-Maps用来正反收集论点（what makes / what does NOT make a good role model），RoleCards用来给comment提供正反例子与peer-feedback讨论素材。

## 2. 争议/辨析 (Aufgabenart 1.1 + AFB)

### 常见正确理解
- Summary与outline不同：summary收全部主要信息并压缩，outline只收题目限定的某些方面（"Outline …/Point out …"即带限定词的summary）。
- Analysis不写作者说了什么（那是summary的事），只写作者做了什么+效果是什么；题目没点的焦点不写。
- Comment的论点必须解释（explain why it supports my position），光罗列不给分；例子要具体（如role cards类素材），不能只有空观点。

### 典型错概念
- Summary里夹带个人评论、用原文整句拼贴、用过去时讲故事——三者都是summary失分重灾区。
- Analysis每段只有Point+Evidence没有Explain（效果分析缺席）=半分答案；Conclusion加新论点=格式错。
- Comment只站一边、没有counterargument；结尾没有final judgement；字数远超/不足（150–200词是纪律）。

### Stellungnahme-Satz (evaluate)
- `A good comment does not list opinions, it earns its judgement: position plus explained reasons plus concrete examples plus one fair counterargument.`

## 3. English Klausur-Sätze (可直接套用)

- Summary首句：`The newspaper article "..." by ... (2026) deals with the influence role models have on young people and their identity.`
- Summary主体：`According to the author, ...; moreover, she stresses the fact that ... . In the final part, she concludes that ... .`
- Analysis-thesis：`In the article, the author uses various stylistic devices to highlight the questionable influence role models can have on young people and their identity.`
- Analysis类型判定：`As the author shares her opinion based on a personal experience, the article is an opinion piece.`
- P.E.E.-Point：`The author employs a negatively connotated word field to ... (cf. l. ...).`
- P.E.E.-Explain：`This word choice arouses the reader's interest and supports the impression that ... .`
- P.E.E.-Explain：`By quoting an expert (l. ...), the author adds validity to her line of argumentation.`
- Analysis收束：`Overall, the author's use of ... emphasises ... and encourages the reader to ... .`
- assess：`Assessing the author's line of argument, the most convincing point is ... because ... .`
- assess：`Assessed against the criterion of fairness, the article is convincing where ..., but one-sided where ... .`
- evaluate：`Evaluating the author's solution critically, I find it convincing/unconvincing because ... .`
- evaluate：`On balance, I evaluate ... as ... since the advantages of ... outweigh the risks of ... .`
- discuss：`Discussing whether the best role models show strengths and weaknesses, I would argue that ... .`
- discuss：`While it is true that ..., I still hold that ... because ... .`
- Comment表态：`In my opinion, role models are most helpful when they show both strengths and weaknesses.`
- Comment让步：`Although perfect role models seem inspiring, they put young people under pressure.`
