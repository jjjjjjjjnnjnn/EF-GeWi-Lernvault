# Englisch IQB Writing Training: Summary → Analysis → Comment (Mini-Text Nigeria/Identitaet, ORIGINAL)

> 中文一句话理解：照 IQB 的三任务格式（概述→分析→评论二选一），用一篇自写的尼日利亚身份短文全程走一遍：任务 + 解答案子 + 评分自查表，练完 Teil A 就有手感。
>
> Lesson v3（9步制）导航：1 ZIELE → 2 PRETRAINING → 3 BEISPIEL → 4 中文讲一遍 → 5 争议/辨析 → 6 Klausur-Saetze → 7 VERGLEICH → 8 FEHLVORSTELLUNG + check/ANTWORT → 9 TAKEAWAY + REFLEXION。方法细节见 Role-Models-Analysis.md（本课不重复）；IQB-Schreiben (nicht-literarisch) 只作格式借鉴（改述），不复制原题原文。

## ZIELE: 本课学完能… (3条)

- [ ] 能概述短文的身份主题要点（Operator：outline/sum up, AFB I — *Outline the author's view on identity.*）
- [ ] 能分析作者塑造身份的手段及其效果（Operator：analyse, AFB II — *Analyse how the author portrays…*）
- [ ] 能二选一评论（assess vs discuss）并用评分表自查（Operator：comment, AFB III — *Assess… / Discuss…*）

## BEISPIEL: Mini-Text + Aufgaben (worked example，先例后题)

### BEISPIEL-Text (English, ORIGINAL, 210 Woerter, alle Personen fiktiv)

> **Between Lagos and London** (practice text written for this note, 2026)
>
> (1) My grandmother in Lagos calls me "our London girl". My classmates in London call me "the Nigerian girl". For a long time I corrected nobody, because I did not know which label was mine.
>
> (2) I was born in London, but I grew up with Yoruba proverbs at breakfast and BBC news at dinner. When I visit Lagos in the summer, the market women praise my English and laugh at my Yoruba. When I return to London, teachers praise my "exotic background" and then expect me to explain a whole continent. Both cities give me a mirror, but each mirror shows only half of my face.
>
> (3) Social media made the split worse and better at the same time. On TikTok I follow Nigerian comedians who joke about exactly this double life, and suddenly I feel less alone. Yet the same platforms push me into boxes: the algorithm decides whether I am "African content" or "British lifestyle". I have started to answer with my own videos, mixing both languages in one sentence. The comments prove that thousands of teenagers live in the same in-between space.
>
> (4) Identity, I have learned, is not a passport stamp. It is a playlist: Lagos drums, London grime, and something new in the middle that is mine alone.

### Aufgaben (Format nach IQB-Vorbild, paraphrasiert)

- Task 1 — outline (AFB I): *Outline the author's view on her identity between Lagos and London.*
- Task 2 — analyse (AFB II): *Analyse how the author portrays her split identity. Focus on imagery and contrast.*
- Task 3 — Wahl, eine von zwei (AFB III):
  - 3.1 *Taking the sentence "Identity is a playlist" (para. 4) as a starting point, assess to what extent a flexible identity helps young people today.* oder
  - 3.2 *Write a blog entry for an international youth magazine in which you discuss opportunities and risks of social media for the identity of teenagers.*

## 1. 中文讲一遍 (Feynman)

- Task 1（概述）：四要素首句（opinion piece + 标题 + 自写作者 + 主题）+ 4 个要点压缩（双标签/两面镜子/社交媒体双刃/playlist 结论），约原文 1/4，不评论。
- Task 2（分析）：只分析题点的两个焦点 — (1) imagery（mirror / playlist / boxes：把抽象身份变具体，读者能"看见"分裂与融合）；(2) contrast（Lagos vs London 两组平行句：结构对称凸显"两边都不完整"）。每点走 P.E.E.，证据标段号（para. 2/3），效果落到读者（sympathy, understanding）。
- Task 3（评论）：3.1 assess 是判一个句子的对错（flexible identity 利弊 + 例子 + 判断）；3.2 discuss + re-creation 是议两面（opportunities vs risks）并装进 blog 格式（标题 + Hi everyone + 结尾提问）。两者都要 counterargument + final judgement。
- 新题（同类，独立做）：用本 Mini-Text 做 Task 2，只分析 first-person narrator 的效果（authenticity），见下方 check。

## 2. 争议/辨析 (Sprachen: Aufgabenart + AFB)

### Pro / 常见正确理解

- Task 1 是 AFB I（wiedergeben），Task 2 是 AFB II（Mittel + Wirkung），Task 3 是 AFB III（urteilen）——顺序固定，不可提前评论。
- assess 判一个给定观点（以它为起点），discuss 议一个开放问题（两面都要）。

### Contra / 典型错概念

- "Task 2 复述故事内容"——错：内容是 Task 1 的事，Task 2 只写手段 + 效果。
- "Task 3 只站一边猛写"——错：无 counterargument 的评论缺公平性，直接丢 AFB-III 分。

### Stellungnahme-Satz (comment)

- *A strong Task 3 does not repeat the text, it judges it: one clear position, explained reasons, one concrete example, one fair counterargument.*

## 3. English Klausur-Saetze (kopierfertig)

- Task 1 首句：`The opinion piece "Between Lagos and London" (practice text, 2026) deals with a young woman's search for identity between Nigeria and Britain.`
- Task 1 主体：`According to the author, both cities label her, yet neither label fits completely; moreover, social media deepens and eases the split at the same time.`
- Task 2 thesis：`The author uses imagery and contrast to make her split identity visible and to win the reader's sympathy.`
- P.E.E.-Point：`The mirror image (para. 2) visualises her feeling of being incomplete on both sides.`
- P.E.E.-Explain：`This contrast structures the whole text and supports the impression that identity must be built, not inherited.`
- assess：`Assessing the playlist metaphor, its strongest point is flexibility, because it allows change without losing roots.`
- discuss：`Discussing opportunities and risks of social media, I would argue that community helps, while algorithms narrow identity.`
- re-creation (blog)：`Hi everyone! Have you ever felt half in one culture and half in another? Here is what I think…`

## 5. Quelle / Aufgabe — Musterloesungsskizze + Bewertungs-Checkliste

### Musterloesungsskizze (Stichpunkte, keine Volltexte)

- Task 1 Skizze: dual labels (para. 1) / two half mirrors Lagos vs London (para. 2) / social media worse + better, algorithm boxes vs own videos (para. 3) / conclusion playlist, identity built not inherited (para. 4). Neutral, present tense, ca. 50-60 Woerter.
- Task 2 Skizze: (a) imagery — mirror (para. 2): P = visual metaphor, E = "each mirror shows only half of my face", E = reader sees incompleteness, sympathy; playlist (para. 4): mixing cultures as music, hopeful tone. (b) contrast — parallel Lagos/London sentences (para. 2), antithesis worse/better (para. 3): symmetrical structure underlines in-between position; conclusion: devices invite identification, no new info.
- Task 3.1 Skizze: Position — flexible identity helps, but needs anchors. Pro: adapts to migration/global youth (example: bilingual teens). Contra: flexibility without roots can feel rootless (peer pressure, algorithm boxes). Final judgement: playlist yes, but with fixed tracks (family, language).
- Task 3.2 Skizze: Blog — title + Hi everyone + opportunities (community, role models, own voice) + risks (boxes, comparison pressure) + counter + question ending.

## VERGLEICH: Task 1 outline vs Task 2 analyse (interleaving + discrimination)

- A题（Task 1, outline）：*Outline the author's view on her identity.*（AFB I：说什么，中性压缩）
- B题（Task 2, analyse）：*Analyse how she portrays it (imagery + contrast).*（AFB II：怎么做，有何效果）
- 二选程序（先选再做）：“问的是内容还是手段？因为问说了什么，所以选 A 收要点；因为问怎么写的，所以选 B 走 P.E.E.…”
- 一句话区别（A vs B）：Outline reproduces WHAT the author says, analyse explains HOW she says it and what effect it has.

## FEHLVORSTELLUNG: 常见误解 + 纠偏

- 误解1：“Task 1 把原文佳句抄下来最保险” → 纠偏：拼贴=own-words 零分（正确：全程改述，只标段号不整句抄）。
- 误解2：“Task 3 写成第二个 summary 也能拿内容分” → 纠偏：重复文本=AFB III 零分（正确：必须判断，position + reasons + counterargument）。

## TAKEAWAY: 1盒总结

> Dreischritt an einem Text：outline 收什么 + analyse 析手段效果 + comment 判观点；可用句：*Outline reproduces what, analyse explains how, comment judges whether.*
